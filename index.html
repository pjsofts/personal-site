PJ Voice

An AI phone agent that does real work: it answers calls, looks up the caller and books, moves or cancels appointments. Correctness is enforced in code, not left to the prompt. It comes with a free, seven-chapter course on building voice agents.

  • Simulated-caller evals and a tutorial you can finish in an hour
  • Phone, browser and text from one engine
